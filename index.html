Study with people in your actual classes

Orbit matches university students by the courses they're actually taking and the hours they're actually free. Add your classes, mark when you're around, and see the people in your exact courses who are free when you are.

Match by classStudents in your exact courses, ranked by how much your free time overlaps. Group study chatsA chat for every course you add, plus direct messages with anyone you match with. Deadlines & sessionsTrack what's due, plan study sessions, and invite the people you match with.

Who it's for

Orbit is open to UBC students to begin with — signing up needs a UBC email address, and every account is confirmed by email. More campuses follow.

Orbit is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the University of British Columbia.

Getting the app

Orbit is being prepared for the App Store. If you've been sent this page by a classmate, check back shortly — or email us and we'll tell you the moment it's live.

Safety comes first

Orbit connects you with people you don't know yet. Every profile and every conversation has report and block, blocking is instant and works both ways, and we review reports of objectionable content and abusive users within 24 hours. Please read the safety guidelines before meeting anyone — meet in public, on campus, and tell a friend where you're going.
