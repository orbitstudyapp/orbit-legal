  5. Impersonate anyone. Don't pretend to be another person, a professor, a TA, university staff, or an Orbit representative. Don't use someone else's photo as your profile picture.
  6. Dox anyone. Don't post or share another person's private information — home address, phone number, class schedule, student number, workplace, family details, or anything else they didn't choose to share on Orbit.
  7. Solicit. No recruiting, no MLM, no promoting a business, no crypto, no "opportunities," no fundraising, no political campaigning, no asking users for money, and no selling tutoring services, notes, or essay-writing help.

7. Reporting, and our 24-hour commitment

Report anything that breaks section 5. There's a report option on every profile and in every conversation, and you can email orbitstudyapp@gmail.com.

8. Safety — please read this

Orbit does not verify who anyone is.

To be completely clear about what we do and don't do:

  • We do not run background checks or criminal record checks on any user.
  • We do not verify identity. We don't check government ID.
  • We do not verify that anyone is actually enrolled at any university.
  • We do not verify age beyond asking you to confirm you're 19+.
  • The "verified" badge only means a UBC email address was confirmed. Email addresses can be shared, kept after graduation, or belong to someone other than who you expect.

Anyone can say anything on their profile. Treat every person on Orbit as a stranger from the internet, because that's what they are until you've actually met them.

Meeting someone from Orbit in person is entirely at your own risk. We don't arrange, supervise, attend, or take responsibility for meetings between users. What happens when you meet someone is between you and them.

Before you meet anyone:

  • Meet in a public place on campus. A library, a study commons, a busy café. Never someone's home, never a private room, never an empty building late at night.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you expect to be done.
  • Keep the first session short.
  • Don't share your home address, your full class schedule, or personal contact details until you actually trust the person.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation, a reason, or a polite exit.
  • If you feel unsafe, get help. On UBC Vancouver campus, call Campus Security at 604-822-2222, or use Safewalk at 604-822-5355. In an emergency, call 911.

Read the full Safety Guidelines in the app for more.

If someone makes you uncomfortable, block and report them. You will never get in trouble for reporting someone.
